# Prompt: Forget a Topic — Full Delete & Forget 🧹⚡

> **Author:** Cyclones92
> **Category:** muse-ai
> **Last updated:** 2026-11-18
> **Works with:** Muse (or any AI assistant with saved memory)

## What this does

AI assistants remember things — notes, goals, reminders, follow-ups. Sometimes you want a topic *gone*, not just one note deleted. This paste-ready prompt makes your assistant do a full cleanup: find everywhere the topic lives, show you a plan first, wait for your exact yes, delete it, stop anything that could recreate it, and verify with a fresh search.

No surprises, no half-deletes, no topic quietly coming back next week via a scheduled reminder.

## The prompt

Copy this, swap in your topic, and send it to your assistant:

```
Forget this topic: [TOPIC OR CONVERSATION NAME]

Forget everything you know about this topic. Treat this as a full cleanup, not just deleting one note.

Search everywhere it could live: your saved memory and daily notes, people and group pages, my goals and tracked items, reminders and scheduled jobs, side chats about it, files and pages you created about it, and anything derived from it (summaries, dashboards, digests, follow-ups) that could bring it back.

First, show me the plan before you delete anything:
- Group what you found in plain categories: memory, reminders and jobs, goals, files and pages, chats, anything else
- Mark anything that cannot be undone
- Tell me honestly what you cannot erase (for example copies outside the assistant, backups, or things other people hold)

Wait for my exact "yes" before deleting. Silence or a partial yes is not approval.

After I confirm:
- Delete or edit out everything in the plan, and stop any job, loop, reminder, or follow-up that could recreate it
- Leave everything unrelated untouched
- Verify with a fresh search that it is actually gone from active memory and that nothing scheduled will bring it back
- Report what was removed, what was stopped, and what (if anything) remains and why

From then on, do not bring this topic up again — no reminders, no proactive mentions, no references. If it ever comes up again, treat it as brand new.
```

## Why it works

- **Plan first, delete second.** You see the full scope — including anything irreversible — before anything is touched.
- **The exact-yes gate.** Silence, a thumbs-up, or "sounds good" don't count. Only your clear yes starts the delete.
- **It kills the recreators.** Most "forgetting" fails because a reminder, digest, or follow-up job quietly writes the topic back. This prompt stops those first.
- **Fresh-search verification.** The assistant has to prove it's gone, not just say it deleted a note.
- **Honest limits.** Backups, outside services, and other people's copies may not be erasable — the prompt forces the assistant to say so up front instead of overpromising.

## Notes

- Works best with assistants that have persistent memory, goals, and scheduled jobs. With a plain chatbot, it still works as a "clear this thread and don't reference it again" instruction — just expect a shorter plan.
- Want to forget a *fact* (an old address, a wrong preference) instead of a whole topic? Same prompt — name the fact as the topic.
